Privacy policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

Olive Assistant ("Olive") is a personal tool. Alex Young built it, and he is its only user. It signs in only to his own Google accounts.

What Olive accesses

With the owner's permission, Olive reads and manages data in his Google accounts, such as Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Contacts and Tasks. It uses that data only to sort, summarise and file the owner's own email, events and notes.

Where the data goes

  • Olive runs on the owner's own computer and may store retrieved content and the results it produces there.
  • Following the owner's instructions, including automated tasks he has set up, Olive sends selected Google-account content to Anthropic's Claude service to be summarised and classified. That content leaves the owner's computer and is handled under the terms and retention settings of the owner's Anthropic account.
  • Olive does not sell Google user data and does not use it for advertising.

Google API data

Olive's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Withdrawing access stops new access. It does not erase copies already stored on the owner's computer or held by Anthropic under its retention terms.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/calexyoung/calexyoung.github.io. Issues are public, so please do not include personal information.
